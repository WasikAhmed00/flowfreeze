# FlowFreeze UPAY-inspired MFS UI refinement

## Design direction

- **Design movement:** restrained digital-banking operations console: navy trust surfaces, sunflower action highlights, white evidence cards, and clear status semantics.
- **Core principles:** progressive disclosure, one primary action per view, financial data legibility, and safety-first feedback.
- **Color philosophy:** deep navy communicates trust and account security; sunflower yellow marks primary actions, focus, and attention; cool white and pale blue keep tables readable; green/amber/red remain reserved for risk states.
- **Layout paradigm:** persistent operations rail with a compact top context bar, then a single dominant task surface rather than many competing cards.
- **Signature elements:** sunflower F-mark, navy rail, BDT amount emphasis, and explicit sandbox/no-live-funds badges.
- **Interaction philosophy:** every button either navigates, refreshes, submits, selects, or explains; disabled states are avoided unless the reason is visible; forms provide immediate validation and success/error feedback.
- **Typography:** existing compact system sans hierarchy retained for data density, with navy headings and generous button hit areas.
- **Brand essence:** UPAY BD risk operations for reviewing synthetic wallet flows before proportionate action; calm, precise, accountable.
- **Brand voice:** direct and operational. Example lines: “Follow the flow. Protect the wallet.” and “Evidence before action.”
- **Wordmark/mark:** retain the FlowFreeze F mark, recolored as sunflower on a navy surface.
- **Signature brand color:** sunflower yellow `#f5c84b`.

## Scope

- Preserve synthetic-only behavior and no-live-wallet boundary.
- Add functional sign-in, create-account, and Google demo sign-in paths.
- Keep existing case review, graph selection, simulation, decision logging, and evaluation actions working.
- Refresh the public preview and update the current feature branch pull request.
